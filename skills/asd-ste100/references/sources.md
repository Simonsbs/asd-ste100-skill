# Sources and precedence

Use the requested edition of the standard as the rule and dictionary authority.
Use current ASD/STEMG pages for access, maintenance, training, and tools guidance.
Publisher documentation and research provide context. Community implementations
can suggest techniques but cannot authorize words or overrule the standard.

## Source conflicts and access

Legacy official `about.html`, `faq.html`, and `request.html` refer to Issue 8.
Treat them as historical. Use current edition-specific evidence when they conflict
with the active pages. A proposed change or future publication date is not an
adopted rule. Do not infer a universal regulatory requirement from a general
overview: verify the applicable contract, authority, and document separately.

The official site links a Zenodo record licensed CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and states that
the standard's copyright notice and special usage rights continue to apply. The
independent skill's license does not extend to these publications. Keep the full
standard, dictionary extracts, and third-party training materials outside public
repositories unless separate rights have been established.

If a source blocks access, say what was inaccessible. Try the official DOI or an
authorized local copy, and do not describe an unsuccessful retrieval as a check
showing no changes. A mirror or search excerpt does not by itself verify a rule.
